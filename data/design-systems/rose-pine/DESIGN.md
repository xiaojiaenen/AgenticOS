## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Rose gold elegance meets deep plum canvas. Warm rose (`#ebbcba`), soft lavender, and pale cyan accents on a deep mauve background (`#191724`). Not cold like blue themes, not aggressive like neon — this is the dark theme for beauty and craft. Gradients are soft and romantic. Corners are rounded. The palette evokes sunset reflecting on water.

**Mood:** Elegant, warm, creative, refined.
**Best for:** Fashion, beauty, lifestyle, wellness, design portfolios, creative agencies.

## 3. Typography

- **Display:** Inter or system sans-serif, 600-700 weight, text-1 or accent
- **Body:** 400 weight, text-2, 18-24px
- **Code:** JetBrains Mono, 14-16px, accent-3
- Rounded, soft feel; generous border-radius (12-20px)

## 4. Prompt Guide

- Rose (`--accent`) is the hero — use it for the most important numbers and titles
- Lavender (`--accent-2`) for secondary emphasis, cyan (`--accent-3`) for contrast
- Gradients should flow from rose → lavender → cyan, echoing a sunset
- Cards float on `--surface` with soft edges, never hard black borders
