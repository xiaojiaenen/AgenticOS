## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

The legendary reading-optimized palette by Ethan Schoonover. Warm sand canvas (`#fdf6e3`) with teal, blue, and violet accents. Scientifically designed for comfortable long-form reading with precisely calibrated contrast ratios. Warm backgrounds dominate, cool accents provide crisp focus. Every color pair was tested for accessibility and reduced eye strain.

**Mood:** Scholarly, precise, timeless, accessible.
**Best for:** Research papers, documentation, legal presentations, technical reports, academic conferences.

## 3. Typography

- **Display:** Inter or system sans-serif, 600-700 weight, text-1
- **Body:** 400 weight, text-2, 18-24px — these contrast ratios are proven optimal
- **Code:** JetBrains Mono, 14-16px, accent
- The body text color (#586e75) is the key — dark enough for readability, light enough for comfort

## 4. Prompt Guide

- Blue (`--accent`) and teal (`--accent-2`) are the primary accent pair
- Violet (`--accent-3`) reserved for rare tertiary emphasis
- Every color has a precise role; don't mix accent colors within a single slide
- Parchment background + white cards = classic academic layout
