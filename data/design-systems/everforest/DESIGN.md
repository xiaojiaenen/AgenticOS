## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere

Forest floor in morning light. Warm parchment canvas (`#fdf6e3`) with sage green and earthy brown accents. Natural, grounding, calm. No artificial neon, no harsh contrast — every color feels like it grew there. The light theme for people who find pure white fatiguing. Parchment tint reduces eye strain while maintaining readability.

**Mood:** Natural, calm, grounded, trustworthy.
**Best for:** Sustainability reports, healthcare, education, agriculture, wellness, long-form presentations.

## 3. Typography

- **Display:** Inter or system sans-serif, 600-700 weight, text-1
- **Body:** 400 weight, text-2, 18-24px
- **Code:** JetBrains Mono, 14-16px, accent
- Generous line-height (1.5+) for natural reading rhythm

## 4. Prompt Guide

- Text is dark green (`--text-1`), not black — this is the key to the organic feel
- Cards are white (`--surface`) on parchment (`--bg`), creating a layered paper effect
- Green accents (`--accent`, `--good`) feel native and expected
- Use `--warn` (amber) and `--bad` (soft red) sparingly as signal colors
